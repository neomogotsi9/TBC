Welcome to Rue's Beauty Studio

Healthy hair is beautiful hair

Our passion is helping every client achieve and maintain strong, healthy hair with care tailored to both natural virgin hair and chemically processed hair.

We take a gentle, personalised approach — starting with understanding your hair's unique needs — using safe, high-quality products and treatments that nourish both hair and scalp for long-term hair health.

Our services include braiding in all styles, wash and twist for locs, hair and scalp treatments, sew-ins, and hair relaxing, alongside facial treatments and waxing — a complete beauty experience under one roof.

Frequently asked questions

What hair services does Rue's Beauty Studio offer?

Rue's Beauty Studio offers braiding, loc services, hair treatments, scalp treatments, sew-ins, hair relaxing, facial treatments and waxing.

Do you offer different types of braids?

Yes. Rue's Beauty Studio offers a range of braiding styles. You can contact the studio to discuss the hairstyle you want before booking.

Do you offer hair treatments and scalp treatments?

Yes. Hair and scalp treatments are available to help care for your hair and scalp. The appropriate treatment can be discussed when making your appointment.

Where is Rue's Beauty Studio located?

Rue's Beauty Studio is located in Florauna, Pretoria North, within the Crown & Claw shared beauty space.

How can I book a hair appointment?

You can book your appointment by contacting Rue's Beauty Studio on WhatsApp.
