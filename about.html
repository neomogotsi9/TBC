Our Story

One space, two crafts.

Rue's Beauty Studio and Tall Lee the Nail Tech are built on a foundation of friendship, passion, and a shared dream.

Their journey began in 2022, when Ruth, founder of Rue's Beauty Studio, and Tall Lee met at a business conference. What started as a conversation between two beauty professionals grew into a genuine friendship — they discovered they shared the same values: a commitment to excellence, a passion for helping clients look and feel their best, and a vision of a beauty space where professionalism and exceptional service come first.

After years of supporting and inspiring one another, they officially launched their joint venture in 2026, bringing together Rue's Beauty Studio's expertise in professional hair care and Tall Lee's talent in nail artistry.

Today their partnership is built on trust, respect, and a genuine love for what they do — ensuring every client receives outstanding service in a welcoming, relaxing environment. Beauty is more than a service here; it's an experience.
