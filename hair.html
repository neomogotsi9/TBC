Welcome to Rue's Beauty Studio

Healthy hair is beautiful hair

Our passion is helping every client achieve and maintain strong, healthy hair with care tailored to both natural virgin hair and chemically processed hair.

We take a gentle, personalised approach — starting with understanding your hair's unique needs — using safe, high-quality products and treatments that nourish both hair and scalp for long-term hair health.

Our services include braiding in all styles, wash and twist for locs, hair and scalp treatments, sew-ins, and hair relaxing, alongside facial treatments and waxing — a complete beauty experience under one roof.
