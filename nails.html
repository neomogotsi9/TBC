Our Story

Founded in 2020

Tall Lee the Nail Tech was born from a passion for beauty, creativity, and helping clients feel confident through beautifully crafted nails. Our mission has always been high-quality nail services in a welcoming, relaxing experience.

As a professionally trained nail technician, Tall Lee combines technical expertise with creativity to deliver beautiful, long-lasting results — with the highest standards of hygiene, quality products, and the latest nail trends and techniques.

Every appointment is tailored to the client's individual style and nail care needs, so each person leaves feeling confident, pampered, and satisfied.

What to Expect

  • Strict hygiene and sanitized tools for every client
